Dissertation

Probabilistic and Generative Modeling for Emotion Recognition and Synthesis

Degree
PhD in Computer Science, University of Hamburg
Defended
12 February 2026
Supervisors

Prof. Nale Lehmann-Willenbrock

Current AI systems still lack social intelligence: the ability to perceive and express the social signals that make collaboration work. The dissertation focuses on affect, and studies it in three settings.

  1. 1

    Individual-level affect recognition

    Treating disagreement between annotators as information, with Bayesian neural networks and Student's t-distributions.

    Interspeech 2022ACII 2022IEEE TAFFC 2023

  2. 2

    Group-level affect recognition

    Annotating group affect over time and modelling how a group's mood converges and diverges.

    Oxford University Press 2025IEEE TAFFC 2026

  3. 3

    Affect synthesis

    Converting the emotion of in-the-wild speech without parallel data, with vocoders, diffusion and duration modelling.

    ITG 2023ICASSP 2024IEEE ASRU 2025
